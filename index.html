Questions families ask us

Who will visit my parents?

One named coordinator who is introduced to you and to your parents before any visit, so nobody arrives as a stranger.

Do you give medical advice?

No. We help with appointments, reports, pharmacy and hospital logistics. Medical decisions stay with doctors.

What happens in an emergency?

We go to your parents, coordinate with the hospital and family, and update you straight away. We work alongside emergency services and never replace them.

Which cities do you cover?

We are starting in a few cities and expanding. Tell us where your parents live on the form and we will confirm.

How does payment work?

A single yearly fee in US dollars. Details are shared with waitlist families before launch.

Will my parents be comfortable with this?

We start with a video introduction and go at their pace. The aim is a friendly presence, not a service call.
